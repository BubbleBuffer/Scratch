A · Patchwork

Signature idea: colour is the structure. Like the calico's unequal patches, every space owns one patch colour, and that patch follows its pages everywhere: sidebar tab, home patch, page header, icon and checkbox.

Today is the big milk-white patch of the cat; the spaces are smaller patches sewn around it, never a uniform card grid. Seams mark real ownership changes, and finishing something sews it shut.

Proposed features shown

  • Space colours (patch per space), with a patch picker on new pages
  • Patchwork home: Today agenda from calendar plus due and overdue tasks
  • Quick capture into Today (bottom bar on phone)
  • Pinned page per space with progress and trip countdown
  • Daily note link
  • Sew-shut completion for checklists and for Today
