  • Chip: inline, in a sentence. Tap shows the summary in a sheet.
  • Chapter card: stacked sheets behind it; opens to the summary, decision and its sections.
  • Section card: one part of a chapter; labelled Chat › Chapter, titled by the section.
  • Quote: folds to one line; opens to the full text.
