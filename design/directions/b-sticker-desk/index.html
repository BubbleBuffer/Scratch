      B · Sticker desk

      Personality through objects: every page wears a die-cut sticker, and home is a desk of real things, while the text itself stays quiet.

      What it shows

      • Page icons are chunky stickers with a milk cut line and a resting tilt. They wobble on hover and stamp down when a page is made.
      • Home is a desk: a sticky note for capture, a torn-off day card for today, stamped tiles for recent pages, and labelled drawers for spaces.
      • The play sits around the text. Body copy, lists and properties are plain Atkinson on calm cloth.
      • Finishing a whole checklist stamps it DONE and throws a small burst of sticker confetti. Single ticks only get a springy check.

      Proposed features (tagged “proposed”)

      • Today card: calendar events plus tasks due today and overdue, with a “now” line.
      • Quick capture: the sticky note drops notes or tasks into an Inbox; docked above the tab bar on mobile.
      • Drawers: spaces with their own colour, sticker, page and task counts, and progress.
      • Sticker sheet: the empty page offers starting points as stickers to peel.
      • Daily note in the + menu, and an Inbox in the sidebar.
