C · Stacks

Signature idea: pages are sheets of paper. Opening one slides it over the last, and the sheets underneath stay visible as edges, so the breadcrumb is the stack.

How it plays

  • Dark duvet rail holds the spaces; content sits on milk sheets on a cloth desk.
  • Click a sheet's edge (or press Esc) to lift the sheets above it. On mobile, tap a top edge, drag the sheet down, or use the bottom button.
  • Ticked to-dos slide down into a "done" pile with a count. Finishing a checklist makes its sheet settle.
  • Toggles unfold like folded paper; inline databases pull out like drawers.
  • Depth comes from the family's buffer-seam shadows, used only on sheet edges and lifted objects.

Proposed features (not in the app yet)

  • Today: calendar agenda as a day line, plus tasks due and overdue.
  • Week strip with per-day dots.
  • Progress rings and bars for checklists and projects.
  • Quick capture that drops a new sheet on the stack.
  • Done pile for completed to-dos; the stack navigator itself.
  • Blank sheets that vanish when lifted away unused.
